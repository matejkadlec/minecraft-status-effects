1. Overview

This website (“the Site”) is an interactive reference of Minecraft status effects. It doesn't use cookies, analytics, advertising or any other tracking, and it doesn't load anything from third-party servers: fonts, icons and scripts are all served by the Site itself.

2. Data controller

The Site is operated by {{ config.AUTHOR }}. Contact: Discord.

3. Data stored in your browser

To remember your settings, the Site saves these keys in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device and you can delete them at any time by clearing the site data in your browser:

  • se-theme – light or dark mode
  • se-sidebar – whether the sidebar is collapsed
  • se-version – the selected Minecraft version
  • se-search – your last search
  • se-filters – the quick filters
  • se-page-length – rows per page
  • se-ignore-filters – the “Ignore filters” export option

4. Server logs

Like any web server, the server may keep standard access logs (IP address, browser user agent, time and requested page) for security and troubleshooting. They are not used to identify or profile visitors and are deleted automatically after a short time (at most 30 days).

The Site is delivered through the network of Cloudflare, Inc., which passes every request on to the server. To do that and to protect the Site from attacks, Cloudflare processes the same technical data (including the IP address) under its privacy policy.

5. Exports

When you export the table, your browser sends the selected version, the theme and the list of effects in the table to the server, which generates the file. Nothing of it is stored.

6. Cookies

The Site sets no cookies, so there is no cookie banner.

7. Legal basis and your rights

The only personal data processed are the server logs above, based on the legitimate interest in running the Site securely. Under the GDPR you can ask for access to or deletion of data about you; in practice this only concerns server logs, if they still exist. Contact me via the link above.

8. Changes

Changes to this policy will update the “Last updated” date above.
