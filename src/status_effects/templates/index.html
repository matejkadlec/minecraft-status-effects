Reading the descriptions

  • level is the effect level: I = 1, II = 2 and so on. 2 × level is 2 at level I, 4 at level II…; 2level grows exponentially, the actual values follow in brackets.
  • Highlighted numbers and names are the important values: amounts, intervals and other effects.
  • Damage is in game points (1 damage = half a heart); healing is in hearts.
  • Max is the highest level you can get in survival. It can differ with the mods in your modpack (some mods raise vanilla limits), so the highest known value is shown.
  • When an effect has no exact numbers, the formula couldn't be found in the mod; “higher level → stronger” means the effect scales with its level.
  • ⚠️ marks effects that are buggy or whose behaviour isn't confirmed.
  • A badge like 1.20.5+ next to an effect name means it exists only from that patch of the selected version.
  • Some effects are added by several mods; the table shows the most popular one.

Reading the sources

  • Potion/Arrow/Charm of X means the potion, its splash and lingering versions, tipped arrows and the Apotheosis potion charm. Bamboo Spikes are Supplementaries' spikes tipped with the potion.
  • Affix Items are Apotheosis items with an affix that applies the effect.
  • Delights are meals and drinks from Farmer's Delight and its addons.
  • Unique Weapons are the unique weapons from Simply Swords.
  • Items, mobs and spells of the effect's own mod are named directly (“spells from the mod”); other mods are written in italics.
  • To be added. means the sources haven't been researched yet, Unavailable. that the effect can't be obtained in survival.
